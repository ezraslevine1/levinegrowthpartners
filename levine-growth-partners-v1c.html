About
EL

Operator. Builder. Collector.

Ezra Levine is a serial entrepreneur and strategic operator with deep roots in the sports collectibles, authentication, and passion asset industries. Through Levine Growth Partners, he partners with founder-led companies to accelerate growth, build lasting platforms, and unlock value at the intersection of culture and capital.

With experience spanning fractional ownership platforms, grading authentication, auction house strategy, youth sports infrastructure, and M&A execution, Ezra brings a rare combination of Wall Street rigor and collector-community credibility to every engagement.
